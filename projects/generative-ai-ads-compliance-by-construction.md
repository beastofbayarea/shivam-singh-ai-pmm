# Generative AI Ads — Compliance-by-Construction

> **Portfolio lens:** AI product marketing, regulated commercialization, trust-led positioning, and advertiser adoption.

## Executive snapshot

Reframed an AI-assisted advertising workflow so compliance shaped creative development before launch instead of rejecting completed work. The product combined policy retrieval, visible coaching, constrained generation, joint copy-and-image inspection, and human escalation to make safe creation faster and commercially stronger.

## Resume-ready impact

- Moved policy upstream in an AI-assisted advertising workflow, reducing rejection from 18% to 1.8% and compliant launch time from five to seven days to under one hour.
- Converted more than 500 pages of legal guidance into tagged, context-retrievable rules and introduced confidence-based enforcement that reduced false positives to 1.4%.
- Validated the system through a 10,000-ad replay, a 500-seller pilot, and a 5,000-ad shadow comparison; constrained categories improved ROAS by more than 40%.

## Interview story

### Situation

Advertisers could create copy and static assets quickly, but 15–20% of submissions were rejected after the work was complete. Product, Growth, Brand, Policy, Legal, and Operations optimized different metrics, leaving no shared definition of a successful launch.

### Task

Create a credible commercial proposition that preserved creative performance while making policy understandable, maintainable, and enforceable inside the product experience.

### Actions

- Aligned stakeholders around successful compliant launches rather than generation speed, click-through rate, or review volume in isolation.
- Structured category, jurisdiction, claim-type, severity, and brand rules so only relevant constraints entered each creative workflow.
- Replaced silent correction with plain-language coaching, safe alternatives, minimal-element repair, and a fast appeal route.
- Used staged validation and confidence thresholds so high-risk violations were blocked, ambiguous cases reached humans, and low-risk categories received lighter treatment.

### Results

- Rejection fell from 18% to 1.8%.
- False positives reached approximately 1.4%.
- Compliant launch time fell below one hour.
- ROAS in constrained high-risk categories improved by more than 40% without a material decline in standard categories.

## Decisions and trade-offs

- Optimize for successful compliant launches, not maximum creative autonomy.
- Make policy a customer-facing product capability rather than a downstream legal gate.
- Preserve human review for ambiguity while automating high-confidence decisions.

## Leadership signal

Unified commercial, product, legal, policy, brand, and operations teams around one outcome and one operating cadence, turning a trust problem into a differentiated value proposition.
