## Evidence-grounded premise

The FTC states that advertising claims must be truthful, non-deceptive, and evidence-based. NIST's GenAI Profile provides a lifecycle structure for identifying and managing risks unique to generative systems. Those primary sources support moving substantiation and policy controls into creative generation rather than relying only on downstream review.

## Case approach

- Convert applicable advertising rules into retrievable, versioned policy objects with clear ownership.
- Require evidence links for material claims and display the relevant rule during drafting.
- Use confidence thresholds: block clear violations, escalate ambiguity, and permit low-risk content with monitoring.
- Evaluate text and imagery together and preserve human review, appeals, and an auditable decision trail.

## Evidence-based success measures

Use substantiation coverage, violation escape rate, false-positive rate, review time, appeal outcomes, and compliant-launch rate. These are proposed measures, not reported historical results.
